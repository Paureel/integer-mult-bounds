\subsection{An orthogonal-star replacement for the complex network}
\label{sec:orthogonal-star}
Retain the paired bit primitive and the compact-control movement, layout,
repair and precision arguments. Only the complex side circuit is replaced.
The construction below was developed in Aurel Prosz's research conversation
with ChatGPT and independently reconstructed and audited with Codex. The
original conversation archive was unavailable during this integration;
the supplied implementation is the checked reconstruction described here.

Put $h=25$, $F=\mathbb F_2^{25}$ with its ordinary dot product,
$v=\binom{25}{3}=2300$, $m=h^3=15625$, and $N=v^3$.
Partition the ground points into consecutive parts of sizes 12 and 13.
Assign each triple to its same-part pair when it meets both parts, and to
its two smallest points when it lies in one part. These are 144 disjoint
groups of source triples. In a group with pair $\{i,j\}$, its indicators are
$u_k=e_i+e_j+e_k$. Over $\mathbb F_2$,
\[
 u_k\cdot u_\ell=\delta_{k\ell}.
\]
Every subset is therefore an orthonormal family. Its span is nondegenerate;
between nested subsets the residual is spanned by the added indicators.

For target triple $S$, request from each group the sum of its triples
disjoint from $S$ with coefficient $1/2$, and the sum of its triples meeting
$S$ in two points with coefficient $-1/2$. Omit empty sums. The retained
center computation contributes $(|S\cap T|-1)/2$ from source $T$.
Adding the requested side coefficient gives one for $S=T$ and zero for every
other pair. This is an identity over the Gaussian-dyadic scalar ring;
binary indicators specify address frames, not the scalar arithmetic.

\paragraph{The alternating residual and its repair.}
For a requested sum with source span $U\subset t_S^\perp$, the injection
residual is $E=t_S^\perp\cap U^\perp$. In a binary symmetric space the
canonical vector represents the functional $x\mapsto x\cdot x$.
In the ambient space it is $\mathbf1$. Since the $u_k$ are orthonormal
and orthogonal to $t_S$, the canonical vector of $E$ is
\[
 \mathbf1+t_S+\sum_{k\in K}u_k.
\]
Thus a nonzero $E$ is alternating precisely when this vector is zero.
Split every offending requested sum into one singleton and the remaining
sum, keeping their common coefficient. The latter canonical vector changes
by the removed nonzero $u_k$. The singleton's vector also cannot vanish,
because the original sum consists of more than one independent indicator.
Both repaired residuals are consequently nonalternating. Exact enumeration
finds 50 such repairs, taking 323940 requests to 323990 partial outputs.
For example, pair $\{0,1\}$ and target $\{0,1,2\}$ with its 22 outside
third points has a two-dimensional alternating residual before repair.

\paragraph{An explicit shared-sum graph.}
Within each group order its third points increasingly. To build a requested
subset sum, bisect the entire ordered interval at its floor midpoint,
recursively build its nonempty intersections with the halves, and add the
two results when both are nonempty. Intern sums by their exact source subset.
Leaves are source inputs. Construct only nodes feeding requested outputs.
Every addition has disjoint supports, and every node stays in its fixed-pair
group. This deterministic finite graph has 160046 binary additions and
323990 designated output uses. The checker verifies all $v^2=5290000$
scalar coefficients, including required zeros, from the actual graph.

Compile the graph reversibly by making a physical role for each outgoing
use at an input. At an addition reuse its first incoming role for one
outgoing use, retire the second incoming role after adding it, and allocate
fresh roles for all remaining uses. Copy the pivot into the other outgoing
roles. These are invertible additions, with inverse fanout undone first.
There are $2A+O$ outgoing uses and one pivot identification per addition,
so $R=A+O=484036$ roles suffice. Simultaneous inputs are distinct.

Let $\mathcal L$ be the mixer, $\mathcal V$ the source copy,
$\mathcal J$ the signed partial-output injection, and $\mathcal G,\mathcal R$
the retained center gather and scatter. With plus/minus signs denoting
the associated updates, use the chronological schedule
\[
 \mathcal L,-\mathcal J,\mathcal L^{-1},-\mathcal R,
 \mathcal V,\mathcal G,\mathcal R,\mathcal L,\mathcal J,
 \mathcal L^{-1},-\mathcal G,-\mathcal V.
\]
For initial scratch $z$, the side injections cancel
$-\mathcal J\mathcal Lz+\mathcal J\mathcal L(z+\mathcal Vx)$.
Centers cancel in the same way. Cleanup restores $z$ and the centers,
leaving $(\mathcal J\mathcal L\mathcal V+\mathcal R\mathcal G)x=x$
added to the target. Reversing and inverting the schedule subtracts $x$.
The three tensor stages therefore give the original signed bank exchange
$(-Y,X)$ with every auxiliary restored for arbitrary initial values.

\paragraph{Complete forward and reverse frames.}
Use the original stage decomposition $A=B\perp P$, suppressing its future
norm-one line factor $Q$, and write
\[
 D_0=B\otimes F,\quad D_1=A\otimes F,\quad
 D_U=(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes U).
\]
In a forward invocation all early mixers, early side injection and the early
center operation use $D_0$. A source copy uses $D_{\langle t_X\rangle}$;
the middle mixer uses $D_U$ for its node's source span; target injection
uses $D_{t_Y^\perp}$. All cleanup mixers and the final source copy use $D_1$.
Giving the early injection $D_0$, rather than its smaller incoming data-line
label, is essential to avoid extra side decreases. The physical target data
can grow to $D_0$ at that gate within its prescribed stage boundary.

In the reversed invocation the early copy and mixer use $D_0$. The physical
$X$ injection uses $D_{\langle t_X\rangle}$; the middle inverse mixer uses
$D_{U^\perp}$; the physical $Y$ copy uses $D_{t_Y^\perp}$; cleanup uses $D_1$.
The reversed graph has $U_w^\perp\subset U_z^\perp$ whenever the forward
graph has $U_z\subset U_w$. Output orthogonality places each physical target
line inside its output complement. Source endpoints have the exact source
line complement. Every gate has one common frame on all its incidences.

The added nonzero residuals have norm-one vectors. Nested source subsets have
orthonormal difference bases; reversed complements have the same residuals.
Fresh and retired roles require source spans or their complements. No active
node contains all 23 indicators of a full star: a disjoint query omits the
target's outside points, an intersection-two query has at most 22 sources,
and a one-point pair intersection selects at most two. Nodes are subsets
of queries. Their span's canonical vector therefore differs from $\mathbf1$,
so its nonzero complement is nonalternating. The only other new residuals
are the repaired output residuals above. Tensoring with $P,Q$ preserves a
norm-one witness. All common-component, data, center and final scratch
residuals are the original ones: coordinate units outside the earlier/future
triple tensor supports supply their norm-one witnesses. Hence every nonzero
residual has an orthonormal basis by the original binary-space argument.
The exact scratch audit traces 7738184 incidences across both orientations.

The data stage boundaries are unchanged. The only decreases are the $h+1$
center returns per invocation, each losing $h$ dimensions. Separate auxiliary
banks are used at all three stages; no matching or cross-stage reuse is assumed.
There are $3v^2$ invocations, so
\[
 L=3v^2h(h+1)=10315500000,\quad
 W=2N+3v^2(R+h+1)=7706397940000.
\]
The signed dimension sum telescopes to $Wm-2N$. Adding twice the total
decrease gives
\[
 s=Wm-2N+2L=120412464109500000,
 \qquad \eta=\frac{Wm-s}{Wm}=\frac{14}{455245625}.
\]

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:orthogonal-star-interface}
The replacement network satisfies the retained complex phase interface,
with these $m,W,s$, the original all-role endpoint corrections, signed bank
exchange and arbitrary-scratch restoration. It supports
$\sigma=1-318/10^{11}$, while the paired bit primitive retains
$\tau=1-296/10^{11}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The scalar and frame arguments establish the common-frame contract.
Each orthonormal residual basis gives one signed translation kernel per
basis vector by orthogonal weight additivity modulo four. Thus exactly $s$
children are charged. The original weight-27 source/sink and signed-bank
corrections still apply, since all data and scratch endpoints are unchanged.
A positive exponential Taylor sum proves $\log m<966/100$; exact rationals
give $\eta>(318/10^{11})(966/100)$. Therefore
$s/W<m^{1-318/10^{11}}$. The certificate checks $2\le s<m^5$ and bounds
the new finite scalar updates within $E=64(W+m+1)^3$. Inverse/endpoint phases
retain their fixed node accounting. The generalized stopping-depth guard
and compact-control layer consequently apply with the replacement constants.
No direct-swap bit argument or new Gaussian-resampling algorithm is used.
\end{proof}
